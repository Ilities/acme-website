title: Five ways to reduce no-shows at your clinic
description: Missed appointments cost small clinics more than most owners realise. These are the changes that make the biggest difference.
pubDate: 2025-09-16
author: Aino Heikkinen
---

A missed 45-minute physiotherapy appointment is not just one lost fee. It is a slot that someone else on the waiting list could have had, and a client whose treatment plan has now slipped by a week.

We looked at booking data from clinics using Acme Book over the past year. Here is what the ones with the lowest no-show rates have in common.

## 1. Send a reminder the day before

This is the single biggest change. Clinics that send an SMS reminder 24 hours ahead see roughly a third fewer missed appointments than those relying on email alone. People read text messages; they do not always read email.

## 2. Make cancelling easy

It sounds backwards, but it works. If cancelling means calling during opening hours, many people just do not turn up. A cancellation link in the reminder gives you the slot back in time to fill it.

## 3. Use a waiting list

A cancellation is only a problem if the slot stays empty. With a waiting list, the next person is offered the time automatically.

## 4. Ask for a deposit on long appointments

For appointments over an hour, or first visits, a small deposit taken at booking makes people think twice before skipping. Most clients do not mind, as long as the cancellation policy is clear.

## 5. Book the next visit before the client leaves

Clients who leave with their next appointment already booked come back far more reliably than those who are told to "book online when it suits you".

None of these are complicated, and you do not have to do all five at once. Starting with reminders and an easy cancellation link covers most of the benefit.
