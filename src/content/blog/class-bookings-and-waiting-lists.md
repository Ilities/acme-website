For a long time Acme Book was built around one-to-one appointments. Over the past year, more and more yoga and pilates studios have asked us to support group classes properly, so that is what we have been working on this spring.

## What is new

- Recurring classes with a fixed capacity, an instructor and a room.
- Waiting lists for full classes. If someone cancels, the first person in line gets the spot and a notification.
- 10-visit passes and monthly memberships, sold online. Visits are deducted automatically when the client books.
- Attendance lists on the instructor's phone, where they can mark people as attended or missed.

## Who gets it

Class bookings and waiting lists are included in the Clinic and Group plans. If you are on Starter and would like to try them, you can switch plans from the account settings and switch back at any time.

## What is next

We are working on letting clients book a series of classes in one go, such as an eight-week beginners course. If that is something you need, we would like to hear how you run your courses today. Write to us at hello@acmebook.example.
