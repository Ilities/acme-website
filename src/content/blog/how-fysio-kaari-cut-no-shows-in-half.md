---
title: How Fysio Kaari cut no-shows in half
description: How a physiotherapy clinic in Kaleva, Tampere cut missed appointments from 30 a month to 14 with SMS reminders and online booking.
pubDate: 2026-11-11
author: Aino Virtanen
---

Fysio Kaari is a physiotherapy clinic in Kaleva, Tampere, with six physiotherapists and one person at reception. Until last spring almost every booking came in by phone.

"Reception spent most of the morning on the phone, and half of the calls were people moving an appointment," says owner Jenni Lahtinen. "The phone rang while we were treating patients, and we still had empty slots in the afternoon."

Fysio Kaari moved to Acme Book in April 2026. Clients now book online from the clinic's website and Google profile. Each physiotherapist has their own treatment lengths, so a first visit gets 60 minutes and a follow-up 45 without anyone checking it by hand. At the end of a visit the physiotherapist books the next one on the spot.

The biggest change came from SMS reminders the day before, with a link to cancel or move the time. Missed appointments went from about 30 a month to 14. Cancelled times go to the waiting list, and most of them get filled the same day.

"Our receptionist now has time for the people who are actually standing at the desk," Jenni says. "And we don't have to chase people who forgot."

Fysio Kaari uses the Clinic plan with SMS reminders and online payments for first visits.
